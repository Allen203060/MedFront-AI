# MedFront Clinic - Insurance & Billing Guidelines

## 1. Accepted Insurance Plans (In-Network)
MedFront Clinic is currently an in-network provider for the following major commercial and government health plans:
- **Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS):** In-network for PPO, EPO, and Traditional indemnity plans. Out-of-network for BCBS Blue Care HMO plans requiring primary care referral unless pre-authorized.
- **Aetna:** In-network for Choice POS II, Open Choice PPO, and Aetna Select.
- **UnitedHealthcare (UHC):** In-network for Choice, Choice Plus, and Medicare Advantage Choice PPO.
- **Cigna:** In-network for Open Access Plus (OAP) and Network PPO plans.
- **Medicare:** In-network for Original Medicare Part B. We accept assignment on all covered Medicare services.
- **Medicaid:** In-network for MassHealth Standard and MassHealth CarePlus. Prior authorization required for specialized diagnostic imaging.

## 2. Out-of-Network & Self-Pay Pricing
For patients without active insurance coverage or choosing to receive out-of-network care:
- **New Patient Consultation (Comprehensive):** $220 flat fee (includes baseline vitals and 45-minute physician evaluation).
- **Established Patient Follow-Up:** $130 flat fee (30-minute consultation).
- **Urgent Care / Same-Day Walk-In Visit:** $160 flat fee.
- **Routine Preventive Annual Checkup:** $180 flat fee (excluding specialized bloodwork/labs).
- **Self-Pay Discount:** A 15% discount is applied if balance is paid in full on the date of service via debit, credit card, or cash.

## 3. Copayment and Deductible Policies
- **Copay Collection:** All applicable specialist or primary care copayments are mandatory and collected at the time of check-in before seeing the physician.
- **High-Deductible Health Plans (HDHP):** If deductible has not been met, patients will be billed following insurance adjudication. Estimated deductible deposit of $75 is collected at check-in.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, HSA/FSA cards, and personal checks.

## 4. Prior Authorizations & Referrals
- Patients enrolled in HMO plans are responsible for obtaining an active referral from their Primary Care Physician (PCP) at least 48 hours before specialty appointments.
- Our prior authorization team coordinates approvals for MRI, CT scans, and specialty medications within 3 to 5 business days.
