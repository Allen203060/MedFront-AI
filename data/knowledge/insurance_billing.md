# MedFront Clinic India - Insurance & Billing Guidelines

## 1. Accepted Insurance Plans & TPAs (Cashless Network)
MedFront Clinic is partnered for direct cashless hospitalisation and outpatient claims with major Indian health insurance providers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs):
- **Star Health and Allied Insurance:** In-network for Star Comprehensive, Young Star, and Senior Citizens Red Carpet health plans.
- **HDFC ERGO General Insurance:** In-network for Optima Secure, My:Health Medisure, and corporate group policies.
- **Care Health Insurance (Religare):** In-network for Care Supreme and Care Advantage plans.
- **ICICI Lombard & Bajaj Allianz:** In-network for complete health insurance and cashless OPD network.
- **Government Healthcare Schemes:** Empanelled under Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) and CGHS for eligible beneficiaries.
- **International & Corporate Expats (Aetna / Cigna / BCBS):** Direct billing supported for Aetna International, Cigna Global, and Blue Cross Blue Shield corporate health cover via overseas assistance desks.

## 2. Out-of-Pocket & Self-Pay Pricing (OPD Consultations)
For self-pay patients and consultations without active insurance cashless coverage:
- **General Medicine / Primary Care Consultation:** ₹800 flat fee (includes baseline vitals and 30-minute physician checkup).
- **Specialist Consultation (Cardiology / Dermatology):** ₹1,200 flat fee (comprehensive evaluation with Dr. Ananya Rao / Dr. Sunita Kapoor).
- **Follow-Up Consultation (Within 7 days):** ₹400 flat fee.
- **Urgent Care / Walk-In Consultation:** ₹1,000 flat fee.
- **Executive Health Preventive Health Checkup:** ₹2,500 flat fee (includes ECG, CBC, lipid profile, HbA1c, and doctor review).

## 3. Payment Methods & Billing Policy
- **Digital Payments:** All major digital payment channels are supported, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa, MasterCard, and Net Banking.
- **Cash Payments:** Cash payments accepted up to government statutory limits at the central billing desk on Ground Floor.
- **Copayments & Deductibles:** Applicable policy deductibles or OPD copayments are settled at check-in prior to doctor consultation.

## 4. Prior Authorizations & Pre-Auth Helpdesk
- Our in-house TPA Insurance Desk assists with cashless pre-authorization within 2 hours for planned daycare and diagnostic procedures.
- Patients must present their physical e-Health Card and Government Photo ID (Aadhaar or PAN Card) at the billing desk.
