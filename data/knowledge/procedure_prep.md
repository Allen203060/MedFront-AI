# MedFront Clinic - Clinical Preparation & Procedure Guidelines

## 1. Fasting Guidelines for Blood Tests
Certain blood panels require strict dietary preparation to guarantee diagnostic accuracy:
- **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) & Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP):** Fasting for 10 to 12 hours prior to blood draw is recommended.
- **Lipid Panel (Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL):** Fasting for 12 hours is mandatory. No food, juices, coffee, or milk.
- **Fasting Blood Glucose / Hemoglobin A1c:** Fasting for 8 hours is required for fasting glucose tests. Plain water is permitted and encouraged.
- **Hydration:** Patients should drink 1 to 2 glasses of plain water before arrival to facilitate venous blood collection.
- **Medications during fasting:** Patients should continue taking routine prescription blood pressure and cardiac medications with a small sip of water unless specifically instructed otherwise by their physician.

## 2. Imaging & Diagnostic Preparation
- **Ultrasound (Abdominal):** Nothing to eat or drink for 8 hours prior to the examination to reduce bowel gas interference.
- **Ultrasound (Pelvic / Renal):** Drink 32 ounces of plain water 1 hour prior to scan; do not empty bladder before the procedure.
- **MRI Screening:** Patients must complete the metal safety screening questionnaire before entering the magnet suite. Notify staff if you have cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators, metal clips, cochlear implants, or occupational metal fragment exposure. Wear loose, comfortable clothing without zippers, metallic threads, or snaps.
- **CT Scan with Contrast:** Fasting for 4 hours prior to scan. Ensure blood creatinine/eGFR laboratory results within the last 30 days are on file to evaluate kidney function before contrast dye administration.

## 3. General Pre-Visit Preparation
- **Documentation to Bring:** Government-issued photo ID, current physical insurance card, list of all current prescription and OTC medications (with dosages), and previous relevant diagnostic imaging discs or reports.
- **Pediatric Patients:** Minor patients under age 18 must be accompanied by a legal parent or guardian for all consultations.
