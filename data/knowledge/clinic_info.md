# MedFront Clinic India - General Information & Operational Policies

## 1. Operating & Opening Hours (Schedule)
The clinic is open for outpatient appointments and visits on the following schedule (Indian Standard Time - IST):
- **Weekdays (Monday to Friday):** Open 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST for routine visits, primary care consultations, and laboratory diagnostic services.
- **Weekends (Saturday):** Open 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM IST (Urgent care walk-ins & scheduled specialist follow-ups only).
- **Weekends (Sunday):** Closed (On-call triage physician available for phone inquiries and medical emergencies).
- **Holidays:** Closed on Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, and Holi.

## 2. Location & Parking
- **Address:** 102, 1st Cross, 5th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560095, India.
- **Public Transit:** Accessible via Bengaluru Namma Metro (Silk Board / Koramangala Station), BMTC Bus routes 201 and G4.
- **Parking:** Dedicated patient parking garage and valet available behind Main Wing B. First 2 hours are complimentary with clinic validation at the reception desk. ₹50/hour thereafter. Wheelchair-accessible (ADA) parking spaces adjacent to the main elevator lobby.

- **Emergency Situations:** If experiencing acute chest pain, breathlessness, sudden numbness, or life-threatening symptoms, patients must immediately dial 112 / 108 or proceed to the nearest emergency room.

## 4. Appointment Policies
- **Arrival Time:** New patients must arrive 15 minutes prior to appointment time for check-in and government ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) verification. Established patients should arrive 10 minutes prior.
- **Cancellation & Rescheduling:** Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a ₹300 late cancellation fee.
- **No-Show Policy:** Three consecutive no-shows without notification may result in temporary suspension of online advance booking.
- **Teleconsultations:** Telehealth consultations are available for non-urgent consultations, routine follow-ups, and lab report reviews via the MedFront Patient Portal app across India.
