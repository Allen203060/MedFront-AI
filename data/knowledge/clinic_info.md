# MedFront Clinic - General Information & Operational Policies

## 1. Operating & Opening Hours (Schedule)
The clinic is open for outpatient appointments and visits on the following schedule:
- **Weekdays (Monday to Friday):** Open 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM EST for routine visits, primary care consultations, and laboratory services.
- **Weekends (Saturday):** Open 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM EST (Urgent care walk-ins & scheduled follow-ups only).
- **Weekends (Sunday):** Closed (On-call triage physician available for phone inquiries and emergencies).
- **Holidays:** Closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

## 2. Location & Parking
- **Address:** 742 Evergreen Medical Pavilion, Suite 400, Springfield, MA 01103
- **Public Transit:** Accessible via Metro Line 2 (Pine Street Station), Bus routes 14 and 27.
- **Parking:** Dedicated patient parking garage located behind Building B. First 2 hours are free with clinic validation at the reception desk. $4/hour thereafter. Accessible (ADA) parking spaces available adjacent to the main entrance elevators.

## 3. Contact & Communication
- **Main Reception Desk:** (555) 019-8234
- **Prescription Refill Line:** (555) 019-8235 (Please allow 48 business hours for standard refill approvals)
- **Fax:** (555) 019-8239
- **Emergency Situations:** If experiencing severe chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden numbness, or life-threatening symptoms, patients must immediately hang up and dial 911 or proceed to the nearest emergency department.

## 4. Appointment Policies
- **Arrival Time:** New patients must arrive 15 minutes prior to appointment time for check-in and document verification. Established patients should arrive 10 minutes prior.
- **Cancellation & Rescheduling:** Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a $50 late cancellation fee.
- **No-Show Policy:** Three consecutive no-shows without notification may result in dismissal from the practice.
- **Telehealth Visits:** Telehealth consultations are available for non-urgent consultations, follow-ups, and routine lab reviews via the MedFront Patient Portal. Patients must be located within the state at the time of the call.
